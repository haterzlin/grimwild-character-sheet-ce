Core Talent

Hex: You can roll Presence to hex someone with: confusion - desire - dread - lethargy - misfortune - pain. Decide when it takes place, or pick a trigger. Once per session, you can ask your patron for a potent hex for free, though each use thereafter carries an upfront cost, forcing you to: take harm - make a sacrifice - roll your patience pool. These are cosmic effects, and not fully under your control.

Your magic comes with strings attached, represented with an 8d Patience pool. Your patron is fickle and expects you to serve their desires. They may lose patience from your requests for power, or when you otherwise displease them. At 0d, your patron will give you a visit to clear up the issue.

[Growth: 2 levels / +1 potent hex without cost per session]

Path Talents

Affliction: Once a session, you can place your hands on another to heal your harm by transferring the injury. Allies can roll defense to resist this effect. Talk with your GM about transferring other maladies, such as conditions.

Aff.

Eldritch Weaponry: You can wield your magic as a weapon freely, using Wits or Presence. On a perfect, you can push yourself to also inflict a secondary effect (as a critical), using your patron's trappings as the touchstone.

Push

Knowing Gaze: When given time, you can learn: the price of their loyalty - their biggest fear - their greatest desire - their next move - what they're hiding - whom they serve. This gaze only works on someone once. You can push yourself to do this on the spot.

Push

Otherworldly Form: You can turn into a floating, dispersed form, flavored after your patron's trappings. Choose three things you can do in this form: appear barely visible - fly high in the sky - interact with objects - move swiftly - slip through small gaps - speak.

Ritualist: Take +1d on a ritual's invocation and replace any one of its anchors with yourself.

Visions: You can open your mind to seek forbidden knowledge. Ask the GM a specific question. The answer may be cryptic or twisted, but it will never be a lie. Make a defense roll against rattled after the answer.

Wayfarer: You can push yourself to teleport. The further or less familiar, the less precise. Max range is two days' ride away. Make a story roll if you can't see the destination or take allies you touch with you.
